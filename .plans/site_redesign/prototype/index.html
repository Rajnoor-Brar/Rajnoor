Abstract. I am a physics master's student. I build tools for simulating particle collisions and write up the mathematics behind my work. The largest of them, Heimdall, runs Monte Carlo studies as chains of supervised processes and scores the results against measured data. Before that I co-authored two papers on how intense laser beams focus themselves in plasma.

Work

Heimdall

A runner for Monte Carlo studies in particle physics. Generators, Rivet and plots run as supervised processes, chained by one TOML file.

Brookhaven

Jet photoproduction with Pythia 8 and Sherpa 3, tested against ZEUS data from HERA before predicting for the Electron-Ion Collider.

Ellipsoidal channel

The surface swept by an ellipsoid whose two foci ride on two space curves, derived and plotted in MATLAB.
